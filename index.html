What it actually does

ScholarOS organises and opens things. It does not write your coursework, and it is not trying to become the place you live.

Reads the page you are on

You sign in to your school yourself, two-factor and all. ScholarOS waits until you are already in, then reads the assignment list that is on your screen. It does not open other pages or crawl your courses.

Shows you every import before it saves

Imports land in a review inbox with a confidence flag and where each field came from. Fix a title, a course, or a due date before it becomes a task. A parser miss is an annoyance rather than a wrong calendar.

Calendar, list, classes, reminders

Drag to reschedule. File classes by term. Group the weekly quizzes that repeat all semester. Soft delete with undo, because everyone mis-taps.

Plans around how you actually work

Smart Plan builds a study schedule from what is due. It learns that you finish one class the night before and another three days early, and schedules accordingly. Runs on a free Google Gemini key you supply.

Remembers which docs belong to which class

Paste a document link once and ScholarOS offers it back on the next assignment in that class.

Opens the whole context at once

One click opens the assignment page, the document you attached, and your AI workspace together, instead of you hunting three tabs.

What it never does

There is no ScholarOS server. No account, no sign-up, no analytics, and nowhere for your work to be sent, because nothing was built to receive it.

✓ Never asks for, stores, or sees your school password
✓ Never touches Duo, SSO, or any multi-factor step
✓ Never runs on any site except the school you added yourself
✓ Never attaches a document without you clicking to confirm it

Uninstalling removes everything. The browser deletes the extension's storage with it, and there is no account to close because none was created. The full detail is in the privacy policy.
